Ongoing Research Projects

Edited Volume

Beyond Empathy: Critical Directions in Health Humanities

Co-edited with Prof. Shazi Shah Jabeen. An interdisciplinary edited volume advancing critical approaches to health humanities beyond empathy through ethical, environmental, cultural, and institutional perspectives. Positive responses received from international contributors; manuscript under development for submission to Bloomsbury (expected February 2027).

Journal Article

Teaching Renewable Energy Critically: Slow Violence and Wind Energy in Indian Environmental Education Textbooks

With Prof. Harpreet Singh Bedi and student collaborators from the Department of Mechanical Engineering. Examines representations of renewable energy, particularly wind energy, in environmental education through the frameworks of slow violence and energy justice. Intended for submission to the Journal of Environmental Education.

Monograph

Indigenous Ecopoetry: Reconnection, Postcolonial Development and Khasi Poets

Develops an environmental humanities framework for interpreting Indigenous ecological knowledge, place, and postcolonial development in contemporary Khasi poetry.

Book Review

Review of Postcolonial Poetry and the Environment: Place, Precarity and Justice (2025) by Pramod K. Nayar

Currently being edited by the Review Editor of the Journal of Postcolonial Writing. Co-authored with doctoral student Rachita Arora as part of a publication-oriented research mentorship.
